# Brag Plan: ErasBus Tarragona

## What is this app?
A free Flutter app that plans the bus between Reus, Tarragona and Salou for Erasmus students, with night buses to clubs, themes and 22 mini games to beat the wait.

## The angle
"Finally on iOS": the app is now on the App Store. Quick tour with real screens: a journey, the night buses, the themes, the games. No invented claims: every line is a headline from the App Store listing (`store/listing.json`) or a real UI string.

## Hook (0-3.7s)
Logo pops, "Finally on iOS." on the app green.

## Key moments
- Journey: home → results (09:15 → 09:40) → trip stops (3.7-8.96s)
- Night buses to favourite clubs (8.96-12.65s)
- Colours, light or dark (12.65-14.76s)
- 22 mini games: arcade → Briscola → Minibus Race (14.76-20.02s)

## Outro
"Now on the App Store." + "Plan your bus. Beat the wait."

## User flow worth showing
Pick stops → see departures → open trip with stops.
